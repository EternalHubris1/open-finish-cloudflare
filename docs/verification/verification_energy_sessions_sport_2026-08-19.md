# Energy invested layout verification

The published Dashboard was verified after commit `0981ef4`.

The Today’s sessions panel is no longer in the hero panel. It is rendered in the upper right side of the Energy invested section, matching the user-designated location. The panel lists current-day sessions with activity icons, activity names, optional notes, durations, and an internal scrollbar when it exceeds its fixed display height. The Return cue remains below it in the lower right portion of the same Energy invested section.

Practice and sport are displayed separately. The main bars and continuity line use practice/work focus minutes only. Sport has an independent, slim horizontal track directly below each daily bar. Its turquoise gradient fill is based on sport minutes against the sport scale and does not alter the practice bar height or the continuity line. The live page loaded with existing Neon data, and no data changed during visual verification.
