# Final Dashboard art direction

Experimental follow-up: [Clean Signal interaction study](CLEAN_SIGNAL.md).

The production Dashboard now combines Concept D's spacious, single-focus composition with Concept E's atmosphere and living mission state.

## What changed

- One current direction and one primary continuation action lead the hero.
- Weekly Progress was removed and replaced by a single-scale Weekly Intensity Timeline.
- Activity color is deliberately absent from the Dashboard timeline; a perceptual infrared scale communicates daily energy only.
- A softly illuminated line accumulates recent effort and decays after quiet days. It is the signature Momentum field, not a score or reward tier.
- Exceptional days receive a slow warm bloom and a calm reflection marker.
- Hover and keyboard focus reveal a daily breakdown; desktop click opens the selected History day.
- On mobile, the first tap focuses the day in the detail sheet, where History remains one explicit action away.
- Loading, empty, stale/error, offline-safe copy, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion states are included.
- General rewards remain a separate system-wide milestone layer; Momentum never replaces Achievements.

## Why

The Dashboard now answers only what happened today, how much energy was invested recently, whether momentum continues, and where to continue. Activity composition and long-term analysis remain in History, preventing the Dashboard from becoming another analytics page.

## Sources

- Concept D: editorial whitespace, one meaningful next action, restrained hierarchy.
- Concept E: mission status, circular momentum signal, ambient depth and trajectory language.
