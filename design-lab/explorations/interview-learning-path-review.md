# Interview learning path · iteration 2026-10-06

Status: implemented local evolution; awaiting user review. Not a replacement of the approved art direction.

## Tension and comparison

The original flat bank mixed runnable author drills with external tasks and offered no prerequisite-aware learning. User requests interview relevance, source research, thematic blocks, and simpler filters.

Compared:

1. Flat company-tagged bank: fast lookup, but unverifiable company frequency and no learning order.
2. Locked course: clear sequencing, but forces completion and obscures legitimate skipping or prior knowledge.
3. Pattern route with optional prerequisites and mixed transfer practice: selected under the user's instruction. More explicit context, no new obligation, existing editable attempts retained.

## Implemented direction

- Traditional existing room hero; restrained data panels for pattern outcomes, progress and controls. No new decorative layer.
- Essential tasks first; optional tasks expand separately. Three visible modes (route, all, repetition), other selections under a disclosure. Only Easy/Medium/Hard filters; Foundation becomes a warmup stage.
- 18 blocks, 88 essential tasks. Prerequisite edges form an ordered DAG. Each block has an observable outcome, recognition cue, invariant and independent self-check.
- Per-task evidence: official interview example type, official employer preparation recommendation, community recruiter recommendation, unverified candidate report, platform task, or original exercise. No generic “asked at company” badge.
- Twelve original Russian adapters turn core external patterns into runnable local practice. Their contracts and limitations are explicit. Other conditions stay on the source site.
- Saved assessments drive progress/recommendations; opened tasks, visible tests and drafts never imply mastery. Every block is accessible; incomplete prerequisites are advisory.
- Mixed practice hides topic labels and opens its selected candidate on entry; existing repeated-attempt outcomes remain editable. Draft-discard guard also applies when switching learning blocks or entering a different mixed task.
