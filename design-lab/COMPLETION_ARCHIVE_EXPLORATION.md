# Completion Archive — experimental product model

Status: prototype, awaiting visual and product approval.

## Recommendation

Treat a fully completed book, course, or substantial block as a manually confirmed artifact of the path. Restore Achievements as its own dōjō room and place completed works beside system Journey Marks as a separate tab. Cabinet remains responsible for reflections, tools, and session evidence.

Keep this archive separate from automatic Journey Marks. System achievements describe rare milestones inferred from activity; completion records preserve what was finished, when, and why it mattered.

## Prototype record

- kind: book, course, or completed work
- title
- creator or source
- completion date
- time invested
- icon or seal
- short personal description

The current hall display uses illustrative records only. Persistence, creation, editing, and evidence links are outside this Design Lab prototype. Dashboard may show compact grouped seals, but full descriptions remain in Achievements.

## Alternatives considered

- Create a nested Cabinet subpage: rejected because it breaks the navigation-as-dōjō-rooms model.
- Keep the archive inside Cabinet: superseded after review because completed works belong with achievements, while Cabinet has a different memory function.
- Reuse automatic achievements: rejected because it blurs earned system milestones with manually curated works.
- Reuse deadlines: rejected because a deadline is a checkpoint, not a durable cultural or learning artifact.

## Next validation

Confirm the archive's place inside Cabinet, card density, vocabulary, and whether each record needs a reflection, source link, rating, or cover image before defining a persistent model.
