# Workflow and memory gates

## Artifact states

- **Proposed:** an idea with rationale; not reusable as a rule.
- **Experimental:** rendered or interactive enough to test; still not canonical.
- **Approved:** explicitly selected and recorded in the Design Bible and decision log.
- **Superseded:** replaced by a newer approved decision; retained for history.

## Gates

For a major direction, require: frame → research → at least three distinct approaches → trade-offs → one recommendation → prototype → responsive and state review → explicit approval → memory update.

For a local change governed by an approved rule, reuse the rule, prototype, review, and record only if the rule itself changes.

## Review questions

- Does this reinforce Personal Operating System rather than task-manager or admin-dashboard behavior?
- Is the surface responsible for orientation now or analysis over time, and is that boundary intact?
- What is immediately legible without instruction?
- Do hierarchy, rhythm, type, color, surfaces, icons, charts, and motion express one intention?
- What happens in loading, empty, error, success, edge, keyboard, touch, narrow-width, and reduced-motion states?
- Which existing approved decision does this evolve?
- If it becomes a new rule, what recurring future decision will it prevent?
